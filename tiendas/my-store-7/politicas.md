## 1) Refund policy (Política de reembolso)

**30-Day Money-Back Guarantee**

We want you and your pet to love your purchase. If you're not completely satisfied, you can request a return within 30 days of receiving your order for a full refund of the product price.

**How to start a return**
1. Email us at alvarochaminademalmierca@gmail.com with your order number and the reason for your return.
2. We'll reply within 1–2 business days with the return instructions and address.
3. Send the item back unused, in its original packaging, with all parts and accessories.

Items sent back without first contacting us cannot be accepted.

**Refunds**
Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Your bank or card provider may need a few extra days to show it.

**Return shipping**
If your item arrived damaged, defective or wrong, we cover the return shipping. For any other reason, return shipping is paid by the customer. Original shipping was free, so nothing is deducted for it.

**Damaged, defective or wrong items**
Please contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the issue. We'll send a free replacement or give you a full refund — your choice.

**Bundle orders**
If you bought the bundle (20% off) and return only one item, we refund the amount you actually paid for that item after the bundle discount.

**Customers in the EU and UK**
In addition to our 30-day guarantee, you have the legal right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason. This does not affect any of your statutory rights.

**Exchanges**
The fastest way to get a different model is to return the item you have and place a new order.

**Questions?** Contact us at alvarochaminademalmierca@gmail.com.

## 2) Shipping policy (Política de envío)

**Free Tracked Shipping Worldwide**

Every order ships free, with tracking, to every country we deliver to.

**Processing time**
Orders are processed within 1–3 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays). You'll receive an email confirmation as soon as your order is placed.

**Delivery time**
Once shipped, orders typically arrive within 7–15 business days. Remote areas and peak holiday periods may take a little longer.

**Tracking**
When your order ships, we'll email you a tracking number so you can follow your package every step of the way.

**Customs, duties and taxes**
Depending on your country, your order may be subject to import duties or taxes. These are set by your local customs authority and, when they can't be collected at checkout, are the responsibility of the customer.

**Wrong address**
Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us as soon as possible — we can update it as long as the order hasn't shipped yet.

**Lost or delayed packages**
If your tracking hasn't updated for 10 business days, or your order hasn't arrived within the expected time, contact us at alvarochaminademalmierca@gmail.com and we'll help you right away with a replacement or refund.
